How to read the dashboard

1

Selected discussions, not survey respondents

Purposive sample; residence and age profiles are not verified.

2

Accounts and clusters are different units

Deduplicate accounts within each theme. Multiple comments can share one video.

3

Observation before interpretation

Self-reports do not verify defects, payment loss, churn or causality.

4

Discovery is not category balance

Equal query effort did not yield category balance. No category winner is established.

No survey or creator interviews were conducted for this scraping study.
